## 共用提示

Create a single original EQUIPMENT RELIC inventory sprite for a mature Chinese folklore pixel dungeon game. The attached game items are STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: hand-clustered deliberate dark stair-step contours, warm brass highlights, inky shadows, selective muted jewel-colored hues. Invent a DIFFERENT item silhouette to match the subject below. True square RGBA PNG with a fully transparent background including all four corners. No lower cast shadow on the canvas, frame, badge, interface, text, other props, aura, smoke, or scattered particles. One centered object fills roughly 78–87% of the square, with slightly oblique three-quarter museum-object view, upper-left amber key, hard sparse highlights and readable silhouette against BOTH a dark fox market and yellowed paper codex. Very low-screen-size readability: the game displays this at 26–44 px. Use two to four clearly separated mass shapes and wide pigment blocks, reserve signature silhouette, avoid overly complex ornate or photographic construction, avoid fuzzy pixels. Space all parts safely away from the image sides. Give materials tangible handcrafted construction.
