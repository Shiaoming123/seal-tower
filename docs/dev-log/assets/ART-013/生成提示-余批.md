# ART-013 其余七张生成提示

沿用首批的像素风格，按 390 宽横幅的取景约束调整主体位置。保存与导出规格见[首批生成提示](生成提示-首批.md)。

Use case: illustration-story. A single wide 16:9 pixel-art scene for a parchment fortune card in an original Chinese dark fantasy dungeon shop RPG. The UI crops to the central up to 3.35:1 horizontal band on short phones: keep the entire central story prop in the middle 42% of the canvas height, away from bottom and top. Strong clear focal silhouette that reads at 280px wide, clustered authentic hand-placed retro RPG pixels, crisp stepped edges, subtle ordered dithering, limited deep petrol teal and weathered walnut shadows with small warm amber highlights. Perspective and overlapping shapes give depth. Low details away from focus, no modern objects, no letters, no text or inscriptions, no logo, no watermark, no border, full-bleed opaque background.

- `omen-calm` A plain smooth pale bamboo fortune slip lies horizontally on a dark wooden window sill, tied with a thin old red cord at one end, next to a small unlit teacup. Dust motes and quiet warm amber light graze the bamboo. Empty tower hall receding behind. Only the center slip catches the light. Blank bamboo, no marks. Strong quiet horizontal silhouette.
- `omen-market` A rich fox-market counter bursting with distinct trade wares: a neat row of rolled silk, low ceramic jars, shallow baskets and warm oil lamps. An open market doorway and teal lattice awnings recede. Amber highlights on the plentiful stocked shelves, contrast with the single parcel of the scarcity illustration. Emphasize the table of goods, no people, no signs.
- `omen-horde` A group of small dark silhouettes, low demon shapes with paired glowing mustard and red eyes, crowding up broad dark tower steps toward camera. The center line has several distinct silhouettes without anatomical detail, backed by a muted rust haze and repetitive stone columns. A single red accent and strongly readable many eyes in the central strip. No gore.
- `omen-moon` An open weathered carved square lattice window with a large pale cool-blue full moon clearly visible through its open center. Moon in the middle horizontal strip, fully within the safe area. Angular moonlight spills onto layered tower stone, quiet blues and muted silver rather than warm gold. Small dark roofline in the distance, no people.
- `omen-fog` An outdoor covered walkway between tower eaves with a small dark silhouette of one hanging square oil lamp glowing weak pale jade-gold in a sea of blue-gray ground mist. Pale sheets of mist part sideways along the central band, broken silhouettes of stone and an unseen far end. Strong contrast between fog bank and scarce oil light. Preserve one unmistakable lamp.
- `omen-festival` Strings of glowing persimmon-red, amber and pale yellow round Chinese paper lanterns hung across a broad interior walkway in layers, with cut-paper rosettes and a few pink petals suspended horizontally in the central safe area. View from eye level across the passage. Emphasize rows of brightly lit lanterns with reflected gold on dark wood, no tables or market wares. No glyphs.
- `omen-bloodmoon` A large deep cinnabar-red full moon looming through a wide carved stone tower arch, fully visible near the center of the safe band. A blue-black roof ridge and mountains silhouetted underneath, low vermilion clouds framing the disk. The disk is round and bright but textured, with a couple of carefully placed red glints on dark stone. No violence or gore. Red moon as the single bright shape.
